# Things I Was Wrong About

Tracking changed beliefs — proof of growth.

## Money Equals Success
- **Old belief**: You always need money to be successful. Financial wealth is the measure of achievement.
- **What changed**: Money is necessary for survival and comfort, but it doesn't define success. Impact, growth, and living aligned with your values matter more.
- **Lesson**: Redefining success on your own terms is more powerful than chasing someone else's scoreboard.
