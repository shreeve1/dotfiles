# Predictions

## AI Won't Fully Replace Jobs — But AI-Skilled People Will Replace Others
AI probably won't eliminate entire job categories wholesale. But within every job category, people who know how to leverage AI will outperform and eventually replace those who don't. The competitive advantage isn't AI itself — it's the human who wields it effectively.

## MSPs Will Need AI to Survive
The MSP model is labor-intensive and margin-thin. AI-driven ticket triage, automated diagnostics, and centralized management aren't luxuries — they'll be survival requirements. MSPs that don't adopt AI will lose to those that do.

## CLI and Code-Based AI Tools Will Win (Uncertain)
Directionally likely — CLI tools and APIs offer more power than GUIs. Power users who can build with these tools will have outsized impact. But this prediction needs research and validation. The market could go differently if GUI tools mature faster than expected.

**Status**: Needs research — how is the AI tooling landscape actually evolving?

## The "AI Person" Role Will Become Standard (Uncertain)
Just as every company eventually needed an "IT person," every company may need someone who understands AI operations — how to deploy, integrate, secure, and manage AI systems. This role doesn't fully exist yet in most companies. But whether this becomes a distinct role vs. an expected skill for existing roles is unclear.

**Status**: Needs research — what AI-specific roles are actually emerging in the job market?
