Books that have shaped James's thinking and growth.

## Never Split the Difference
- **Author**: Chris Voss
- **Why it matters**: Helped James grow as a person. Technical skills came naturally — talking to and interacting with people was the harder challenge. This book bridged that gap.
- **Key takeaway**: Negotiation and communication are skills that can be learned systematically, just like technology.
- **Impact area**: People skills, management, communication
