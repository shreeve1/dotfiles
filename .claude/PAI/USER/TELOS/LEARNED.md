# Lessons Learned

Hard-won truths from career and life.

## Always Double Check Your Work
No matter how confident you are in your solution, verify it. A quick review catches mistakes that hours of debugging won't.

## There's Always Someone Smarter
Humility is a professional asset. No matter how skilled you become, someone out there knows more. Stay open to learning from anyone.

## The Fastest Way to Learn Is to Fail
Failure isn't the opposite of progress — it's the mechanism of progress. Build things, break things, learn from what went wrong.

## Technical Skills Are the Easy Part
For James, technology came naturally. The real growth area was learning to communicate, interact with people, and eventually lead them. The soft skills are the hard skills.

## Don't Wait to Start
Procrastination on committing to tech cost years. The lesson: when you know what you want, commit immediately. Waiting doesn't make you more ready.

## Automate Repetition
If you find yourself doing something a second time, that's your signal to automate it. Human attention is too valuable for repetitive tasks.
