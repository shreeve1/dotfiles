Not tutorials or courses — real systems with real users. ITAStack serves the actual MSP. Agent Zero is deployed for the actual team. Be the person at work (and in the industry) who understands AI deeply. Build AI systems, lead AI decisions, demonstrate value through working products. When businesses figure out how they'll use AI, be the person they call.

## Build the Portfolio Through Work
Every project at IT Assurance is also a portfolio piece. ITAStack proves integration architecture. Agent Zero proves AI deployment. The homelab proves infrastructure depth. The work IS the resume.

## CLI-First Power Usage
Operate through command-line tools for speed and depth. Claude Code, shell scripting, automation pipelines. This isn't just preference — it's a competitive advantage in the AI era where CLI tools offer more power than GUIs.

## Bridge Technical and Human
Use management experience to translate between technical capability and business value. The ability to build AND explain is rare and valuable.
