# Wisdom

## Do the Work
There is no shortcut. Show up, build, iterate. The gap between wanting something and having it is doing the work.

## Always Double Check
No matter how confident you are, verify. Smart people make mistakes too. The extra minute of checking saves hours of fixing.

## Money Is Not Success
You need money to survive and live, but accumulating it is not the same as succeeding. Success is defined by impact, growth, and alignment with your values.

## Start Before You're Ready
Waiting until you're "ready" is procrastination wearing a mask. The best time to start was earlier. The second best time is now.

## Technical Skill Is Necessary but Not Sufficient
Being good at technology is the floor, not the ceiling. Learning to communicate, lead, and connect with people is what multiplies technical ability.

## The First Step Is Figuring Out the First Step
When facing a complex problem, don't try to solve it all at once. Identify the process, find the entry point, and start there.
