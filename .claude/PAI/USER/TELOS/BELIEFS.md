# Core Beliefs

## The Golden Rule
Do unto others as you would have them do unto you. This is the foundational principle — it applies to management, to building technology, to life.

## Learn by Doing
The fastest way to understand something is to build it. Theory is useful but production experience is where real knowledge lives. Build first, refine later.

## Systems Over Heroics
Everything is a system or process. The first step to solving any problem is figuring out what the first step is. Work the process.

## Automate the Repeated
If you do something twice, automate it. Human time is too valuable for repetitive work.

## Fail Fast, Learn Faster
The fastest way to learn is to fail. Mistakes aren't setbacks — they're data. Double check your work, but don't fear getting it wrong.

## Humility in Skill
No matter how smart you are, there's probably someone smarter. Stay humble, stay learning.

## AI Augments, Doesn't Replace
AI probably won't replace people's jobs completely, but someone who knows how to use AI will replace someone who doesn't. Be the person who knows how to use AI.
