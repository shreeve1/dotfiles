# Problems to Solve

## How to Scale MSP Operations with AI
The core business problem. MSPs are labor-intensive — every client issue requires human attention. AI can triage, research, and potentially resolve issues faster. ITAStack is the current attempt at this, but the question is: how far can AI-driven automation go in an MSP context? What's the ceiling?

## How to Transition from Ops to an AI Career
The career problem. James has deep operations and infrastructure experience, plus hands-on AI building experience. The specific barriers to entry for AI roles aren't fully mapped yet — this needs research. The portfolio approach (ITAStack, Agent Zero, homelab) is the current bet, but the path from MSP operations to a dedicated AI career isn't clear. What roles exist? What do they require? What's the most efficient bridge?

**Status**: Needs research — what does the AI job market actually look for, and where does James's profile fit?

## Centralized AI Server Management
The technical problem. Managing dozens of client servers individually doesn't scale. The vision: AI agents that can monitor, diagnose, and remediate server issues across all clients from a central location, connected via Tailscale. This is the medium-term goal but the architecture isn't fully figured out yet.

## Making Management Work as a Technical Person
The people problem. First management role after years as the technical escalation point. The skills that made James successful as a sysadmin (deep focus, solo problem-solving, technical depth) aren't the same skills needed for management (delegation, communication, people development).
