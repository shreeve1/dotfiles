# Mission

Make technology accessible for those who struggle with it — and stay ahead of the AI curve to be the person who bridges AI capabilities with real business needs.

## The Why

Technology should serve people, not intimidate them. James got into tech because he saw people struggling with it and wanted to help. That mission hasn't changed — the tools have. AI is the next frontier of making technology work for people, and being the person who understands both the tech and the people is where the leverage is.

## Current Expression

- Building AI integrations that make IT Assurance's MSP operations faster and smarter
- Deploying AI tools for the team so they can serve clients better
- Learning AI deeply through production use, not just theory
- Positioning to make AI a career, not just a side project
