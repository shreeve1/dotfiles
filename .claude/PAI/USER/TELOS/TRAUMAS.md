# Setbacks and Growth Points

## The Late Commitment
Knew early that technology was the path, but didn't commit when it mattered. Studied CS at Mt. Hood Community College (2013-2015) but didn't push into the industry until late 2017. The gap between knowing and doing cost years of career development.

**Impact**: Creates a persistent feeling of being behind peers. Drives urgency and intensity in current work — the need to catch up fuels the pace of building.

**Growth from it**: The late start taught the cost of procrastination firsthand. Now applies "do the work" and "start before you're ready" as core principles. The pain of delay became the motivation for action.

## Always Playing Catch-Up
With ~7-8 years in tech instead of 15+, there's a constant sense that others started earlier, know more, and have deeper credentials. This is amplified in AI, where many practitioners have advanced degrees and research backgrounds.

**Impact**: Can create imposter syndrome despite evidence to the contrary (production K8s clusters, 33-service integration platforms, AI agent deployments).

**Growth from it**: The feeling of being behind is also what keeps the pace high. It's uncomfortable but productive. The key is ensuring it drives action rather than paralysis.
