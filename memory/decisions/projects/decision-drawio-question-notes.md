## Decision

Use yellow notes throughout a diagram's life, not only during brainstorming. Reserve a right-hand column for active questions, decisions needing confirmation, assumptions, and risks. When answered, preserve notes as history by marking, stacking, or consolidating them; make room for new active questions without deleting the answered record.
